Holistic Care & Management Protocol
  • Diet & Nutrients: Folate/Folic acid supplementation (400 mcg daily), antioxidant-rich fruits/vegetables, healthy fats (avocado, nuts), and adequate hydration.
  • Exercise: Moderate cardiovascular exercise (brisk walking, swimming) to support endocrine balance; avoid exhaustive over-training which can suppress ovulation.

Holistic Care & Management Protocol
  • Diet & Nutrients: Prenatal vitamins containing folate, iron, calcium, and DHA; frequent small meals to manage morning sickness; strict avoidance of unpasteurized dairy, raw fish, and alcohol.
  • Exercise: Pregnancy-safe low-impact exercise (prenatal yoga, swimming, walking) as approved by your obstetrician.
  • Rest & Sleep: Sleep on your left side in the second and third trimesters to optimize placental blood flow.
  • When to See a Doctor: Contact your obstetrician immediately if experiencing vaginal bleeding, severe sudden headaches, visual disturbances, facial/hand swelling, sharp abdominal pain, or decreased fetal movement.

Holistic Care & Management Protocol
  • Diet & Nutrients: Easy-to-digest high-energy foods in early labor (broths, crackers, coconut water); stay well-hydrated.
  • Exercise: Pelvic floor conditioning, perineal massage (from 34 weeks per doctor approval), and active birth positioning practice.
  • Rest & Sleep: Prioritize deep rest in the final weeks as nighttime waking increases.
  • When to See a Doctor: Go to the hospital or birth center immediately upon experiencing regular rhythmic contractions (e.g., 5 minutes apart lasting 1 minute for 1 hour), rupture of membranes (water breaking), or any sign of distress.

Holistic Care & Management Protocol
  • Diet & Nutrients: Nutrient-dense postpartum recovery meals, high protein, extra hydration for lactation (if breastfeeding), and continued iron/multivitamin support.
  • Exercise: Gentle postpartum walking and core/pelvic floor re-engagement only after clearance at the 6-week postnatal checkup.
  • Rest & Sleep: Sleep whenever the baby sleeps to counteract sleep deprivation.
  • When to See a Doctor: Seek immediate emergency care for heavy postpartum hemorrhage (soaking >2 pads/hour for 2 consecutive hours), foul-smelling lochia, severe lower abdominal pain, high fever, or signs of postpartum depression/severe mood changes.

Hormonal Health Assessment & Bone Education

Bone Health Focus
Calcium & Vitamin D Optimization
Holistic Care & Management Protocol
  • Diet & Nutrients: Plant-based phytoestrogens (flaxseeds, soy), elemental calcium (1,200 mg/day for women 50+), Vitamin D3 (800–1,000 IU/day), and reducing triggers like caffeine, alcohol, and spicy foods.
  • Exercise: Weight-bearing resistance training, brisk walking, and yoga to preserve bone mineral density and manage vasomotor symptoms.
  • Rest & Sleep: Keep bedroom temperatures cool, wear breathable layers, and practice wind-down breathing exercises for insomnia.
  • When to See a Doctor: Consult a gynecologist or endocrinologist if experiencing postmenopausal bleeding (any bleeding after 12 consecutive months without a period), severe vasomotor symptoms impacting daily life, or for bone mineral density (DEXA) screening.
