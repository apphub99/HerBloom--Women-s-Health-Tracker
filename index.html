Bone Health Focus
Calcium & Vitamin D Optimization

Clinical Education: During perimenopause and postmenopause, declining estrogen levels increase the risk of bone mineral density loss (osteopenia/osteoporosis). Adequate daily intake of elemental calcium (1,200 mg/day for women 50+), Vitamin D3 (800–1000 IU/day), and weight-bearing exercise are strongly recommended.
